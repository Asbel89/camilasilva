Profile

Brazilian samba, carried to the world stage.

Camila Silva is an internationally experienced Brazilian samba dancer, instructor and cultural ambassador with over 21 years of professional experience in Carnival, stage performance and international cultural exchange.

Her work goes beyond performance. Camila teaches samba as a cultural language, connecting technique, rhythm and body expression with the Afro-Brazilian roots of Brazilian Carnival.

With international experience across Europe, Africa, Asia and North America, Camila delivers samba classes, workshops and immersive cultural experiences rooted in Brazilian tradition, designed for Brazilian and non-Brazilian audiences, festivals, cultural institutions and professional dance schools.

Teaching & Method

How Brazilian samba is taught to international audiences

Camila Silva's teaching approach combines technical precision, cultural context and embodied learning, designed for non-Brazilian students and for Brazilian dancers interested in international-level samba education. Her methodology translates samba as a living cultural language — accessible, engaging and respectful of its Afro-Brazilian roots — and has been successfully applied in Europe, Africa, Asia and North America, in workshops, residencies and cultural programs.

1

Technique & Rhythm

Fundamental samba steps, rhythm structure and coordination, adapted to different experience levels and cultural backgrounds.

2

Cultural Context

Understanding samba beyond movement: history, Carnival culture and Afro-Brazilian heritage as part of the learning process.

3

Body Expression & Presence

Posture, fluidity, musical interpretation and confidence, developing authentic samba expression.

4

Progressive Learning

Classes structured from beginner to advanced levels, ensuring clarity, safety and continuous development for international participants and Brazilian dancers seeking advanced training.
